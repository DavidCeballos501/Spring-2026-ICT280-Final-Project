How small offices can save energy

Turning off unused equipment, using timers, and checking energy reports can make a real difference over time.

Start by checking lights, printers, break room equipment, and devices left plugged in overnight.

Why recycling data matters

Smart recycling systems can show a business where waste is happening and where training is needed.

When teams see simple data, they can make better choices about paper, plastic, and reusable materials.

Understanding a carbon footprint

A carbon footprint is the amount of carbon emissions connected to daily activities and business operations.

Tracking transportation, energy use, and waste can help a company set realistic improvement goals.
